// 03 — the big idea

why "whatever"?

Because that's the brief. You bring whatever's broken, slow or impossible. We build whatever fixes it.

We don't sell a product and hope it fits. We don't have a niche, on purpose. A concierge business, a jewellery studio and a clinic all have different problems, so they should all get different tools.

Anything you can download, your competitor can download too. The stuff we build only exists for you.

And if you've already been burned by AI slop (the chatbot that invents answers, the app held together with tape, the images that look a little off), bring it to us. Cleaning that up is a big part of what we do.

off-the-shelf is for shoes. not for your business.

// 09 — faq

questions people actually ask

faq — repl
How much does it cost?

Depends entirely on the problem. A simple automation and a full custom platform are very different builds. Tell us what you need and we'll send a clear quote after the call.

How long does it take?

Small automations can be live in days. Bigger tools take a few weeks. You'll get a real timeline in the plan before anything starts.

I'm not technical. Is that a problem?

That's literally why we exist. Explain it like you'd explain it to a friend. Translating it into tech is our job.

Do you only work with businesses in Bengaluru?

Nope. We're Bengaluru-based but fully remote. If you have WhatsApp and a problem, we can work together.

What if what I need doesn't exist yet?

Even better. That's the whole point of custom.

Someone already built us something with AI and it's a mess. Can you fix it?

Yes, and we kind of love this one. We figure out what's actually broken, keep what works, and rebuild the rest properly. No judgement about how it got that way.

Who owns the thing you build?

You do.

What happens after launch?

We stick around for fixes and changes.
